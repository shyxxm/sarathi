# Standing Instructions — Kochi Homeware Distributors

Account: customer-2
Last revised: 2 January 2026

## Delivery window

*Booked time — I'm late, will they still take it, what happens if I
miss the slot, is there any grace.*

There is a grace period at this account, and it has an edge. A vehicle arriving
up to **30 minutes** after the booked window may still unload, provided a dock
is available.

The grace is the thirty minutes and the dock together. Neither carries the
delivery on its own. Beyond thirty minutes the grace does not apply — report it
and wait for instruction rather than assuming the godown will take it.

## Detention

*Waiting — how long can I wait before it starts costing, who pays for
waiting, when does the clock start, is my waiting time being counted.*

Free waiting time: **60** minutes from arrival.

Beyond that, waiting is charged at **150** paise per minute.

Waiting is counted from the recorded arrival time. A driver's own estimate of
how long he has been waiting is noted but does not determine the charge.

## Gate and security

*Getting in — which gate, what papers do I show, who do I report to,
the gate is locked and nobody is opening it.*

Use the goods entrance. Ring the intercom and show the LR. The vehicle then
waits in the marked bay until the warehouse coordinator assigns a dock.

**A shut gate does not stop the waiting clock.** Waiting for the entrance to be
opened, and waiting for a dock once inside, are both waiting. Counting runs
from the time the arrival is recorded, not from the time the gate opens or the
dock is assigned.

So a driver held at a closed goods entrance is on the same terms as one held in
the bay: **60 minutes free from the recorded arrival, then 150 paise a
minute.** The detention section below is the same rule stated in full; a closed
gate is not a separate case and does not need a separate answer.

## If the consignee is not available

*Nobody there — shop is shut, no answer, who do I call, how long do I
wait before I do something.*

The warehouse coordinator answers the goods entrance intercom. Ring it and
report the delivery. If nobody answers, report it to the office and wait for
instruction.

## Reattempt

*Coming back — can I try again today, or is it tomorrow, how late can I
still return.*

Same-day reattempt is accepted at this account, **before 17:00**.

After 17:00 the office rebooks the delivery for the next working day.

## Site contact

*Who I call at this place.*

Warehouse coordinator, goods entrance intercom.
