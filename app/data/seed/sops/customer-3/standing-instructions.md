# Standing Instructions — Malabar Electrical Supplies

Account: customer-3
Last revised: 28 November 2025

## Delivery window

*Booked time — I'm late, will they still take it, what happens if I
miss the slot, is there any grace.*

Delivery at this account is **by appointment only**, and there is **no
late-arrival grace**. A vehicle that misses its appointment has missed the
delivery, whether or not the site is open when it gets there.

The distinction matters at this account: notice does not buy time here. Nothing
recovers a missed appointment at the site. It goes back to the office for a new
one.

## Detention

*Waiting — how long can I wait before it starts costing, who pays for
waiting, when does the clock start, is my waiting time being counted.*

Free waiting time: **15** minutes from arrival.

Beyond that, waiting is charged at **400** paise per minute.

This is a short free period. Waiting at this account crosses into chargeable
time quickly, and the office should be notified as soon as it does.

## Gate and security

*Getting in — which gate, what papers do I show, who do I report to,
the gate is locked and nobody is opening it.*

Enter through gate two. The stores clerk signs the LR at the delivery counter
before the vehicle moves to the unloading area. The vehicle does not move up
without that signature.

## If the consignee is not available

*Nobody there — shop is shut, no answer, who do I call, how long do I
wait before I do something.*

The stores clerk is at the delivery counter beside gate two. Report it there.
If the counter is unattended, report it to the office and wait for instruction.

## Reattempt

*Coming back — can I try again today, or is it tomorrow, how late can I
still return.*

A reattempt at this account needs a **new appointment**, and that appointment
must be made **before 14:00**. There is no returning to the site on the
strength of the original booking.

A request after 14:00 moves to the next working day.

## Site contact

*Who I call at this place.*

Stores clerk, delivery counter beside gate two.
