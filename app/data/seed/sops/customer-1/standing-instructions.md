# Standing Instructions — Periyar Wholesale Foods

Account: customer-1
Last revised: 14 March 2026

## Delivery window

*Booked time — I'm late, will they still take it, what happens if I
miss the slot, is there any grace.*

Delivery windows at this account are strict. The booked slot is the slot.

Arriving after the window has closed does not end the delivery on its own, but
it does not proceed on its own either: unloading a late vehicle needs the
receiving supervisor's approval. That approval is his to give or to refuse, and
it is asked for at the main gate. If the window is going to be missed, report
it.

## Detention

*Waiting — how long can I wait before it starts costing, who pays for
waiting, when does the clock start, is my waiting time being counted.*

Free waiting time: **30** minutes from arrival at the gate.

Beyond that, waiting is charged at **250** paise per minute.

Waiting time is counted from the time the vehicle reports arrival, not from the
time a problem is reported. Time spent unloading is not waiting time.

## Gate and security

*Getting in — which gate, what papers do I show, who do I report to,
the gate is locked and nobody is opening it.*

Report to the main gate. The driver produces the LR and the invoice. Security
issues a gate-in slip, and unloading does not begin before that slip is in
hand.

## If the consignee is not available

*Nobody there — shop is shut, no answer, who do I call, how long do I
wait before I do something.*

The receiving supervisor is at the main gate security desk. Report it to him
first. If that does not settle it, report it to the office and wait for
instruction.

## Reattempt

*Coming back — can I try again today, or is it tomorrow, how late can I
still return.*

Same-day reattempt is permitted, but the vehicle must **arrive before 16:00**.
A return that cannot reach the gate by then is not a same-day reattempt, and
the office rebooks it. The office arranges the return — the driver does not
negotiate it at the gate.

## Site contact

*Who I call at this place.*

Receiving supervisor, main gate security desk.
